Company Overview

Infrastructure That Serves Communities

Shree Himal Group Byawasaya Udhyog is a Nepal-based construction and infrastructure business focused on construction works that contribute to connectivity, local development, community facilities and built infrastructure.

The company undertakes construction activities including roads, bridges, nala and drainage works, culverts, housing and other local infrastructure development works.

The company approaches each assignment with attention to practical execution, site requirements, workmanship, coordination and completion of the required construction work.

Who We Are

A Nepal-based construction and infrastructure company

Shree Himal Group Byawasaya Udhyog operates in the construction and infrastructure sector, undertaking works that support the development and connectivity needs of local communities across Nepal.

  • Sector of Operation. The company works within the construction and infrastructure development sector, engaging in road, bridge, drainage, culvert, housing and local infrastructure works.

  • Focus on Local Infrastructure. A core part of the company's work is directed toward local infrastructure that supports community access, mobility and everyday functionality.

  • Practical Execution. Each assignment is approached with attention to site conditions, workmanship and the practical requirements needed to complete construction work as assigned.

  • Supporting Connectivity & Development. Through its construction activities, the company contributes to improved connectivity between communities, settlements and essential destinations.

  • Responsible Project Execution. The company maintains a focus on coordination, site requirements and responsible completion of the construction works it undertakes.

What We Do

Construction & infrastructure works we undertake

Shree Himal Group Byawasaya Udhyog engages in the following core areas of construction and infrastructure development.

01

Road Construction

Road construction and development works that support local mobility and connectivity between communities, settlements and important destinations. The work may include construction activities required for practical road development according to project requirements and site conditions.

02

Bridge Construction

Bridge and crossing construction works designed to support movement across natural and constructed barriers. The company undertakes construction activities according to the requirements of the assigned infrastructure project and site conditions.

03

Nala & Drainage

Nala and drainage construction works supporting controlled water flow, drainage management and protection of surrounding roads and infrastructure. Proper drainage is an important part of maintaining functional and resilient local infrastructure.

04

Culvert Construction

Culvert construction works designed to provide safe passage for water beneath roads and other infrastructure. Culverts support road continuity while helping manage natural water flow around construction areas.

05

Housing Construction

Housing and building construction works focused on functional spaces, practical construction requirements, workmanship and appropriate execution according to project needs.

06

Local Infrastructure Development

Construction and infrastructure works supporting local development programs, municipalities, rural municipalities and community infrastructure. Such works can contribute to improved accessibility, facilities and public infrastructure.
