Our Background

About Shree Himal Group

Shree Himal Group began its business journey with its registered roots in Sandhikharka-05, Arghakhanchi. The business has continued to develop its activities around construction, infrastructure support, material supply and practical project services.

From its registered origin in Arghakhanchi, the business has developed its operations over time and currently operates from Murgiya, Sainamaina-04, Rupandehi, supporting construction and infrastructure requirements in the surrounding area.

The business maintains a practical approach to construction services, construction material supply and equipment-related support, with an emphasis on dependable service and responsible business practices.
