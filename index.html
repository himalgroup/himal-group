About Shree Himal Group

Established with its roots in Sandhikharka, Arghakhanchi, Shree Himal Group has continued to develop its business activities with a focus on construction, infrastructure support, material supply and dependable project services. From its registered origin in Arghakhanchi, the business has expanded its operations to Murgiya, Sainamaina-04, Rupandehi, serving construction and infrastructure requirements in the surrounding area.

Business Activities

In addition to construction and infrastructure activities, the business is involved in construction material supply: Gitti • Baluwa • Mixcut. We supply construction-related materials to support local construction and infrastructure requirements.

Equipment & Machinery Support

To make project operations more convenient, Shree Himal Group also supports equipment-related requirements by supplying accessories and necessary items for tipper, excavator and dozer operations.
